🩺Not medical advice

Babyway is a log for parents and caregivers. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment tool, and it is not a substitute for your pediatrician or other health professional.

Growth percentiles compare measurements with the WHO Child Growth Standards. They are for information only. They can be incomplete or not a good fit for every baby.

Feeding reminders are a convenience. They can be late, missed, or wrong if a feed was logged on a delay or on another phone. Do not rely on them as your only reminder.

Always talk to your pediatrician about your baby's health, growth, feeding, sleep, medicine, or anything you're worried about, before you make a medical decision.
