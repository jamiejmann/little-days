Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Babyway is operated by Jamie Manning, an individual.

The short version
  • We only collect what Babyway needs to work: your login, your name, and the baby details and logs your family enters.
  • Only the family members you invite can see your baby's information.
  • We never sell your data. The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. When something breaks, it sends us a short error report with no personal or baby information, so we can fix it (see “Error reports” below). This website counts anonymous page visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and collects no personal information.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, right in the app.
  • If you sign up on this website (for the free newborn log, the beta or launch news), we store your email and send a few short emails: a welcome series with tips, beta news if you asked for it, and launch news. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we’ll delete your address if you ask.

📋What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password (stored securely as a one-way hash, which we can't read), or, if you use Sign in with Apple on the iPhone app, the email address Apple provides (which may be a private relay) and, on the first sign-in, the name you choose to share. We also store the name you choose to show your family.
  • Baby information: your baby's name, emoji, birth date and time, and, if you add it, sex (used only for the WHO growth charts).
  • Logs: feeds, diapers, sleep, pumping and medicine, and the times they happened, including who logged each one. If you add them, this also includes a pumping side or amount, a medicine name and dose, a poop's color, texture and amount, and a short note on any entry.
  • Growth: weight, length and head measurements.
  • Notes: the short notes you add to entries, and the questions you keep for the pediatrician.
  • Alerts: if you turn on feeding alerts, a push notification token for that phone or browser, your alarm settings and your time zone.
  • Website email sign-ups: if you sign up on this website for the free newborn log, the beta or launch news, we store the email address you enter, which form you used and the time you signed up. We use it only to send Babyway emails: a short welcome series with tips, beta invitations if you asked for the beta, and launch news. We may also see whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, to keep the list healthy. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email.
  • Purchases: if your family buys the Family Unlock, Apple handles the payment. We receive a record that the unlock was bought and which babies it covers, never your card or bank details.
  • Error reports: if something goes wrong in the app, it sends us a short report: the error message (with email addresses, long numbers and IDs removed), the app version, the name of the screen you were on, and a random ID made on your device that isn't linked to your account, your baby or your email. Reports never include your baby's information, your logs or your notes. They're stored with Supabase, only we can read them, and they're deleted automatically after 90 days.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Voice logging (optional): if you tap the microphone and say something like “4 ounces at 2pm,” your words are turned into text so the app can fill in an entry for you to check. In the iPhone app this happens on your phone, using Apple's on-device speech recognition. In a web browser, the browser's own speech feature does it, and some browsers use their maker's servers for that (see your browser's privacy policy). Your voice is never recorded or uploaded to us, and we don't keep the words you said. Only the entry you check and save is kept, like any entry you tap in. The app asks for microphone and speech recognition permission the first time you use it, and you can turn these off at any time in your phone's Settings.

Some things stay only on your own device and are never sent to us, like your unit preferences and entries waiting to sync while you're offline.

💡Why we use it

We use your information only to run Babyway:

  • signing you in
  • showing your family's log on every caregiver's device
  • making reports, growth charts and the pediatrician summary
  • sending the feeding alerts you ask for
  • finding and fixing bugs, using the short error reports described above
  • sending the website emails you signed up for (welcome tips, beta invitations and launch news), until you unsubscribe

We don't use it for advertising or profiling, and we don't use it to train AI models.

👨‍👩‍👧Who can see it

  • Your family: only the people you invite to a baby (with a code you share) can see that baby's information and logs, along with each caregiver's name.
  • Us (the developer): we don't look at your family's data. The only exceptions are when you ask us for help and it's needed to fix your problem, or when the law requires it.
  • No one else: we never sell, rent or trade your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Website emails: these are not family or app data. We use them only to send the Babyway emails you signed up for.

🔒Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States. It's encrypted in transit, and access rules in the database make sure each person can only read the babies they belong to.

A few other services help run the app. They only handle what they need for that job:

  • Feeding alerts are delivered by Apple (iPhone) or by your browser maker's push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple). The alert text, like "Olive's feed is due", passes through them.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads a code library from esm.sh. Like any website host, they may see basic technical details such as your IP address. They don't receive your family's data. If you sign up for emails here, that address is stored with Supabase too, separate from any family's app data.
  • Website emails are sent with Kit (ConvertKit, in the United States), which stores your email address and sign-up details for that purpose only and adds the unsubscribe link to every email.
  • If you join the beta, the test app is delivered through Apple’s TestFlight, under Apple’s privacy policy.

🗑️Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time.

  • In the app, open Family → Settings → Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm.
  • If you haven't set up a baby yet, the option is at the bottom of the welcome screen.

Here's what happens:

  • Your sign-in, name, invite codes and alert tokens are deleted straight away.
  • A baby that only you were on is deleted, along with everything logged for it.
  • A baby you share with others stays with them. If you were the only owner, the caregiver who has been on it longest becomes the owner. Entries you logged there stay for the family and show as "Former caregiver".

You can also ask us to delete your data, or for a copy of it, by emailing hello@getbabyway.com. Any caregiver can download a baby's log as a CSV file from Reports at any time.

If you signed up on this website for emails, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to us at the same address and we’ll delete your address.

Deleted data can stay in our encrypted database backups for up to 7 days before it's gone for good. If a security breach ever affects your family's data, we'll tell you without unreasonable delay, and within 60 days at most.

🍼Children's data

Babyway is made for parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children, and children should not create accounts.

The information about a baby is entered by their parents or the caregivers they invite. The parent stays in control of it: they decide who is invited, and they can remove caregivers or delete the data at any time.

🩺Not medical advice

Growth percentiles and summaries are for information only. They are not medical advice. Always talk to your pediatrician about your baby's health.

🏥Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Some of what families enter in Babyway is consumer health data. This short section is written with Washington's My Health My Data Act in mind, and may also help in other places with similar laws.

What we collect: the health-related information your family types in, including feeds, diapers, sleep, pumping, medicine (name and dose, if you add them), growth measurements, notes, and questions you keep for the pediatrician, together with the baby's name, birth date and time, and optional sex used only for WHO growth charts.

Why we collect it: only to provide the service you asked for — signing you in, showing the shared log, making reports and growth charts, building the pediatrician summary, and sending the feeding alerts you turn on.

Consent: we collect consumer health data only to provide the app you asked for. The app asks for your OK when you create an account, before you log anything. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or emailing us.

Who it is shared with: only the caregivers you invite. Our database provider, Supabase, stores it as our processor on servers in the United States, and may only use it to host the app for us. If you turn on feeding alerts, Apple (on iPhone) or your browser's push service delivers the alert text. We do not sell consumer health data, we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Who receives it, by name: Supabase (our database, United States); Apple (Sign in with Apple, iPhone alerts, purchases and TestFlight); your browser's push service, such as Google, Mozilla or Apple (the text of a feeding alert only). Website emails go through Kit and anonymous visit counts through GoatCounter; neither receives family or health data.

Your rights: you can ask us to confirm whether we collect or share your consumer health data, to see it, to get a list of everyone we've shared it with, to withdraw your consent, or to delete it. Email hello@getbabyway.com and we'll respond within 45 days. We never sell it.

Appeals: if we turn down a request, reply to our answer with the word Appeal and we'll review it within 45 days. If you're still unhappy, you can contact your state attorney general. In Washington, that's www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

How to delete it: in the app, go to Family → Settings → Delete my account and type DELETE, or email us. See “Deleting your data” above. You can also download a baby's log as a CSV from Reports. If you signed up on this website for emails, write to us and we'll delete that too.

Contact: hello@getbabyway.com.
